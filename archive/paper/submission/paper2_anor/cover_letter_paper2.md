# Cover letter — *Annals of Operations Research*

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, **"When Should a Jump Model Switch?
Evidence-Adaptive Transition Costs with Exact Decoding,"** for publication in
*Annals of Operations Research*.

**What the paper does.** It develops a family of *time-varying* transition costs
for the statistical jump model that respond to state-fit evidence while
preserving exact dynamic-programming decoding in $O(TK^2)$. The methodological
core is threefold: (i) an exact time-varying decoder that nests the fixed jump
model at zero adaptation; (ii) a binary value-difference recursion,
$d_t = g_t + \operatorname{clip}(d_{t-1}, -b_t, a_t)$, that exposes a same-day
"amplification" channel in which one observation can influence the terminal
decision more than one-for-one; and (iii) a pair-balanced construction with an
exact directed-cost identity. An empirical study on public U.S., German, and
Japanese proxies then shows a clean separation between *mechanism* behavior
(lagging removes candidate-path whipsaws, pooled 17 → 6) and *selected-strategy*
performance (which improves the fixed model only in Germany). A frozen
path/choice attribution explains why. The contribution is an exact, auditable set
of objectives together with a failure map — not a universal trading rule.

**Fit.** The paper is an operations-research methodology contribution: exact
combinatorial decoding, dynamic programming, and a rigorous separation of
algorithmic mechanism from downstream selection. It extends the jump-model line
of Bemporad et al. (2018), Nystrup et al. (2020, 2021), and Aydınhan et al.
(2024).

**Companion-manuscript disclosure.** A separate, empirically focused manuscript
by the same author — *"One Risk Measure, Fewer Trades: A Parsimonious Jump Model
for U.S. Downside Protection"* — is being submitted to the *Journal of Asset
Management*. The two papers share only the public data set and a concise,
independently worded description of the trading protocol. They do not share an
abstract, a principal figure or table, a theorem, or any substantive paragraph.
The present paper does **not** present the downside-deviation-only empirical
result as a contribution; that companion paper contains **none** of the
adaptive-cost derivations developed here. An overlap audit (identical phrases
longer than 12 words) and a claim-to-reference matrix accompany the project's
supplementary material.

**Format and ethics.** The manuscript is prepared in the official Springer Nature
`sn-jnl` template with APA (author-year) references; all references were verified
against Crossref. It includes funding, competing-interest, data-availability,
code-availability, author-contribution, and AI-assistance statements. The work is
original and not under consideration elsewhere.
